Design Approach

The design of the landing page follows a structured and visually appealing layout. The color scheme primarily features shades of blue, signifying trust and professionalism, complemented by white backgrounds for readability. The typography is clean and modern, ensuring accessibility and ease of reading.

The page is divided into multiple sections:

Hero Section: A large banner with a clear headline and CTA (Call-to-Action) button directing users to the application form.

Why Study MBBS Abroad?: Highlights the benefits of studying abroad using a grid-based layout for clarity.

Top Countries: Showcases the most preferred destinations for MBBS studies, designed as clickable cards.

Admission Process: A step-by-step guide for applicants to understand the process.

Application Form: A simple and structured lead generation form that ensures user input validation.
